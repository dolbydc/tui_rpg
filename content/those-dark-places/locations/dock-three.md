A cargo dock repurposed for the occasional passenger. Pallets of ore sacks wait under a cracked
sign: AUREX COMBINE. EVERY BREATH IS A PARTNERSHIP. An air meter by the hatch ticks over as you
breathe, billing the badge clipped to your coat. The way in leads to the station's concourse.
