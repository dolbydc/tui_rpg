The maintenance ladder ends in the bottom of the bore: a rough rock shaft lit only by the dying
red glow of a work lamp. Drill bits the size of a person lie where they were dropped. At the far end
the rock simply stops, and beyond it is a smooth, dark hollow that was never cut by any drill. The
hum is everywhere down here.
