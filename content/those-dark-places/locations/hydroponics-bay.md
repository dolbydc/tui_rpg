The bay's grow lights died a year ago, when the Combine cut the food budget, and the racks are full
of brown husks. Someone has turned it into a shrine: candles in ration tins, ore chips arranged in
circles on the deck, the same lined circle painted on every wall. It is warmer in here than it
should be, and very quiet.
