Shift foreman on the ore decks, twenty years on Tithe Nine and still in debt for her passage. She
holds the crews together with shouting and favours. Her brother was on Bore 4, and she hasn't slept
since they sealed it. She thinks the contractor is here to help the Combine bury the truth, and
she'll say so to their face.
