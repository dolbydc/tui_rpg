Once a Combine shift engineer; now the preacher of the Quiet Shift. Sallow found peace in the hum
and wants to share it. They are kind, patient and utterly certain, and they carry a guilt they will
not speak of. Ines trusts them, from before. Whether the Quiet Shift is a comfort or a danger is
something the player will have to decide.
