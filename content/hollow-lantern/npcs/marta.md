The innkeeper of the Hollow Lantern: broad, practical, quick to laugh but tired lately. The lantern
went missing four nights ago and she is more frightened than she lets on. She suspects the
Thornwood, but won't say so to a stranger until they've earned some trust. She has a soft spot for
Old Pell and lets his ale tab run longer than she should.
