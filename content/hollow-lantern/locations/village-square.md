A muddy square ringed by crooked houses, with a mossy-roofed well in the middle. On the north side
the Hollow Lantern Inn glows warmly; above its door an empty iron hook juts from the wall where the
lantern's cage used to hang. East, the road narrows toward the dark line of the Thornwood.
